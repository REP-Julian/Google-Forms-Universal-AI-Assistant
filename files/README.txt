Google Form AI Assistant - Knowledge Files Folder
===================================================
Place your course and exam study materials in this folder:
- Microsoft Word documents: .docx
- Adobe PDF documents: .pdf
- PowerPoint presentations: .pptx, .ppt
- Plain text / Markdown notes: .txt, .md

After adding or removing files:
Double-click "sync_files.bat" in the main folder (or click "🔄 Reload files/ Folder" in the HUD).
The AI Assistant will automatically read and ground its answers in all documents placed here!
